---
name: initiative
description: >-
  Take ownership of a broad goal when the user asks for initiative or delegates
  planning and execution, with minimal demands on their attention and inspectable
  results. Examples: "Take initiative on reducing the effort I spend answering
  the agent" and "Own this research goal and choose the workflow and experiments
  yourself". NOT for narrowly assigned tasks, subagent work with fixed
  deliverables, or ordinary questions without a delegation of initiative.
