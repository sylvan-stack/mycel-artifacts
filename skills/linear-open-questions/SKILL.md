---
name: linear-open-questions
description: >-
  Collect every open question and pending decision that waits for the owner
  in the Linear workspace amorphius and give them as one numbered list in
  the session: rows marked Open in a specification's Decisions table, issues
  that ask the owner to decide, and questions agents put to the owner in
  comments. Each item is written so it can be answered in the chat without
  opening Linear: the question in plain words, the proposed answer, the other
  choices and a source link. The user answers by number in the chat instead
  of commenting in Linear, and each answer is then recorded where its
  question is recorded. Example prompts: "/linear-open-questions", "List my
  open questions from Linear so I can answer them here", "Which decisions
  are waiting for me in Linear?" and "Let me answer the open Mycel decisions
  in chat". NOT for publishing or refreshing the Needs your attention entry
  or tidying the sidebar (linear-organize), or for going through the
  comments the owner left in Linear (linear-resolve).
