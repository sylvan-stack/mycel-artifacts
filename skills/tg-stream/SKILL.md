---
name: tg-stream
description: >-
  Activate only when the owner explicitly invokes /tg-stream, or its native
  Codex equivalent $tg-stream. Resolve a named Telegram stream to its current
  members, read or search authorized chat content, or make requested local
  membership changes. Examples: "/tg-stream work summarize today's messages"
  and "/tg-stream show work". NOT for a plain Telegram or stream mention,
  automatic monitoring, Telegram bots, or sending messages/reactions.
---

Read the Telegram streams guide `~/Artifacts/mycel/operations/tg-stream.md` and
follow it for this task.
